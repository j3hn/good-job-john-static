## A practice built across disciplines

My work has moved between graphic design, websites, agency leadership and hands-on production. The common thread is translating an idea into something that works for someone else.

Co-founding In10tion meant moving between design, development and client conversations. At We Are Coast, interface work sat alongside custom websites, infrastructure and the systems that helped the studio deliver.

## Getting closer to the product

With Flowforest, that experience is coming together around independent products. Runway, Flare and Slate explore different ways to capture and work with ideas: files, voice and handwriting.

Agentic development is part of how I’m building them. It brings more of the implementation within reach of the design process, while leaving the product decisions, trade-offs and responsibility with me.

## A working journal

This space follows the work as it develops: the decisions, interface studies and things learned by making. These products are still in progress. That makes this a useful place to show how they take shape.
